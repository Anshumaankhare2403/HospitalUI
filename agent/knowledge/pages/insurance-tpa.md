## 1. Cashless Hospitalization & TPA Desk Workflow
Vasudev Multispeciality Hospital operates a dedicated 24×7 TPA & Insurance Desk in the main reception lobby to facilitate cashless admissions, pre-authorizations, and claim settlements.

### Required Documentation for Cashless Hospitalization
- Patient's Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport).
- Active Health Insurance Card / Policy Document showing policyholder number.
- Ayushman Bharat Golden Card / Ration Card (for government beneficiary schemes).
- Doctor's initial consultation prescription and admission recommendation.
- Diagnostic lab and radiological investigation reports supporting the diagnosis.
