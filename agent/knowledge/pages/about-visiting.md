## 4. Visitor Conduct & Infection Control Rules
1. **Visitor Pass:** All visitors must collect an authorized visitor badge from the front reception counter.
2. **Age Restriction:** Children under 12 years are not permitted in inpatient clinical areas to protect young immune systems.
3. **Hand Hygiene:** Mandatory sanitization with alcohol-based rub before entering and upon leaving patient rooms.
4. **Mask Mandate:** Visitors must wear protective medical masks covering mouth and nose in clinical zones.
5. **Infection Symptom Screening:** Individuals exhibiting fever, cough, flu-like symptoms, or rash are barred from entry.
6. **Mobile Phone Restrictions:** Mobile phone calling is strictly banned in ICU, CCU, and neonatal areas to avoid telemetry and ventilator signal interference.
7. **Photography Prohibition:** Audio, video, and photographic recording are forbidden to respect patient dignity and privacy.
8. **Outside Food & Flowers:** Strictly controlled and requires explicit clearance from the nursing station.
9. **Zero Tolerance Substance Policy:** Tobacco, smoking, paan, and alcohol consumption are strictly prohibited across the entire campus.
