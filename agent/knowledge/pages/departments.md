## Complete List of 16 Clinical Departments

### 1. Nephrology & Dialysis (`nephrology`)
- **Hindi Title:** किडनी रोग का सम्पूर्ण इलाज
- **Icon:** `kidney`
- **Summary:** Complete care for kidney disease, from early diagnosis to long-term dialysis, led by a DM Nephrologist trained at CMC Vellore and the University of Toronto.
- **Conditions Treated:** Chronic kidney disease (CKD), Acute kidney injury, Diabetic and hypertensive kidney disease, Kidney stones (medical management), Swelling, protein or blood in urine, Electrolyte disorders.
- **Procedures Offered:** Haemodialysis (Apollo Dialysis Clinic), CAPD (peritoneal dialysis), Kidney biopsy, Dialysis catheter placement, Pre- and post-transplant care.

### 2. Urology (`urology`)
- **Hindi Title:** मूत्र रोग (पथरी एवं प्रोस्टेट)
- **Icon:** `urology`
- **Summary:** Medical and surgical treatment of stones, prostate and urinary-tract problems by an MCh Urologist.
- **Conditions Treated:** Kidney and ureteric stones, Enlarged prostate (BPH), Urinary tract infection, Urethral stricture, Blood in urine.
- **Procedures Offered:** Ureteroscopy (URS) for stones, PCNL for kidney stones, TURP (prostate surgery), Cystoscopy, DJ stenting.

### 3. General & Laparoscopic Surgery (`general-surgery`)
- **Hindi Title:** जनरल सर्जरी एवं लेप्रोस्कोपी
- **Icon:** `surgery`
- **Summary:** Open and keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery in modular operation theatres with laminar airflow.
- **Conditions Treated:** Gallbladder stones, Appendicitis, Hernia, Piles, fissure and fistula, Hydrocele, Lumps, cysts and abscesses.
- **Procedures Offered:** Laparoscopic gallbladder removal (cholecystectomy), Laparoscopic / open appendix removal, Hernia repair (laparoscopic and open), Piles/fissure/fistula surgery, Hydrocele surgery, Lipoma and cyst excision.

### 4. Obstetrics & Gynaecology (`obstetrics-gynaecology`)
- **Hindi Title:** प्रसूति एवं स्त्री रोग
- **Icon:** `mother`
- **Summary:** Pregnancy care, safe delivery and treatment of women's health problems, with a dedicated labour room.
- **Conditions Treated:** Pregnancy and antenatal check-ups, Irregular or heavy periods, PCOS, Fibroids and ovarian cysts, Menopause problems, Infections.
- **Procedures Offered:** Normal delivery, Caesarean section (C-section), Hysterectomy, D&C, Family planning (tubectomy).

### 5. Orthopaedics (`orthopaedics`)
- **Hindi Title:** हड्डी रोग
- **Icon:** `bone`
- **Summary:** Treatment of fractures, joint pain and sports injuries, including joint replacement surgery.
- **Conditions Treated:** Fractures and dislocations, Knee and hip arthritis, Back and neck pain, Sports and ligament injuries, Frozen shoulder.
- **Procedures Offered:** Fracture fixation (plating, nailing), Knee and hip joint replacement, C-arm guided procedures, Plaster and splinting.

### 6. Neurology (`neurology`)
- **Hindi Title:** मस्तिष्क रोग
- **Icon:** `brain`
- **Summary:** Diagnosis and treatment of stroke, seizures, headaches and nerve disorders by DM Neurologists.
- **Conditions Treated:** Stroke (paralysis), Epilepsy and seizures, Migraine and chronic headache, Parkinson's disease, Nerve pain and neuropathy, Vertigo.
- **Procedures Offered:** Acute stroke management, Seizure management, Neuro-rehabilitation planning.

### 7. Neurosurgery (`neurosurgery`)
- **Hindi Title:** न्यूरोसर्जरी
- **Icon:** `spine`
- **Summary:** Surgical care for head injuries, brain hemorrhage, and spinal cord disorders.
- **Conditions Treated:** Traumatic brain injury, Intracranial hemorrhage, Spine disc herniation, Spinal fractures.
- **Procedures Offered:** Craniotomy, Hematoma evacuation, Spinal decompression and instrumentation.

### 8. General Medicine, Diabetes & BP (`general-medicine`)
- **Hindi Title:** जनरल मेडिसिन एवं मधुमेह
- **Icon:** `stethoscope`
- **Summary:** Everyday and long-term care for fever, infections, diabetes, blood pressure and thyroid problems.
- **Conditions Treated:** Dengue, malaria, typhoid, viral fevers, Type 1 & 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Thyroid disorders, Respiratory infections.
- **Procedures Offered:** Comprehensive health checkups, Diabetic monitoring, Inpatient medical management.

### 9. Radiology & Imaging (`radiology`)
- **Hindi Title:** रेडियोलॉजी एवं सोनोग्राफी
- **Icon:** `scan`
- **Summary:** In-house CT scan, sonography and digital X-ray, reported by an MD, DNB Radiologist.
- **Conditions Treated:** Diagnostic imaging for trauma, abdominal pain, chest pathologies, neurological conditions.
- **Procedures Offered:** Multi-slice CT scan, 2D/3D Ultrasound & Colour Doppler, Digital X-ray, USG-guided procedures.

### 10. Paediatrics (`paediatrics`)
- **Hindi Title:** बाल रोग
- **Icon:** `child`
- **Summary:** Care for newborns, infants and children, including vaccinations and emergency paediatric admissions.
- **Conditions Treated:** Childhood infections, asthma, nutritional deficiencies, jaundice, neonatal illness.
- **Procedures Offered:** National immunization schedule vaccination, Paediatric nebulization, Phototherapy.

### 11. Gastroenterology & Gastro Surgery (`gastroenterology`)
- **Hindi Title:** पेट एवं लिवर रोग
- **Icon:** `stomach`
- **Summary:** Medical and surgical management of digestive diseases, liver disorders, pancreatitis, and GI bleeding.
- **Conditions Treated:** Acid reflux (GERD), Peptic ulcers, Jaundice & Hepatitis, Cirrhosis, Pancreatitis.
- **Procedures Offered:** Endoscopy evaluation, Medical GI management, Surgical interventions.

### 12. Anaesthesia & Critical Care (`anaesthesia-critical-care`)
- **Hindi Title:** निश्चेतना एवं क्रिटिकल केयर
- **Icon:** `icu`
- **Summary:** Safe anaesthesia for every surgical intervention and round-the-clock intensive care unit (ICU) management.
- **Conditions Treated:** Multi-organ failure, Sepsis, ARDS, Post-operative recovery, Severe trauma.
- **Procedures Offered:** Mechanical ventilation, Arterial & central line insertion, Advanced CPR, Epidural analgesia.

### 13. Psychiatry (`psychiatry`)
- **Hindi Title:** मनोचिकित्सा एवं मानसिक स्वास्थ्य
- **Icon:** `mental`
- **Summary:** Confidential care for depression, anxiety, sleep problems, OCD, and substance de-addiction.
- **Conditions Treated:** Clinical depression, Generalized anxiety disorder, Bipolar disorder, Schizophrenia, Insomnia.
- **Procedures Offered:** Pharmacotherapy, Psychotherapy referral, De-addiction counselling.

### 14. Physiotherapy (`physiotherapy`)
- **Hindi Title:** फिजियोथेरेपी एवं पुनर्वास
- **Icon:** `exercise`
- **Summary:** Physical rehabilitation following orthopedic surgery, stroke recovery, sports injuries, and chronic pain.
- **Conditions Treated:** Post-fracture stiffness, Sciatica, Paralysis rehabilitation, Spondylitis.
- **Procedures Offered:** Electrotherapy (IFT, TENS), Ultrasound therapy, Manual therapy, Targeted exercise rehab.

### 15. Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery (`maxillofacial-surgery`)
- **Hindi Title:** मुख एवं जबड़ा सर्जरी
- **Icon:** `dental`
- **Summary:** Surgical correction and trauma reconstruction of jaw fractures, facial lacerations, and oral lesions.
- **Conditions Treated:** Mandible/maxillary fractures, Facial trauma, Impacted wisdom teeth, Cysts of the jaw.
- **Procedures Offered:** Maxillofacial plating, Surgical extraction, Biopsies.

### 16. Ear, Nose & Throat - ENT (`ent`)
- **Hindi Title:** कान, नाक एवं गला रोग
- **Icon:** `ent`
- **Summary:** Medical and surgical treatment for ear infections, hearing loss, chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, and tonsillitis.
- **Conditions Treated:** Otitis media, Deviated nasal septum (DNS), Chronic tonsillitis, Sinusitis, Hoarseness.
- **Procedures Offered:** Otoscopy, Foreign body removal, Tonsillectomy, Septoplasty.
