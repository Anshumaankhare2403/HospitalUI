## 3. Emergency & Trauma Triage Protocol
When an emergency patient arrives:
1. **Zero-Delay Triage:** Immediate admission to the Emergency resuscitation bay without upfront financial bottlenecks.
2. **Duty Doctor & Intensivist Alert:** Duty Emergency Physician and resident ICU intensivist assess ABCs (Airway, Breathing, Circulation) within 60 seconds.
3. **Bedside Diagnostics:** Rapid ECG, portable digital X-ray, point-of-care ultrasound (FAST scan), and STAT blood tests dispatched immediately.
4. **On-Call Specialist Activation:** Emergency nephrologist, laparoscopic surgeon, orthopaedic surgeon, or radiologist paged on Code Green.
