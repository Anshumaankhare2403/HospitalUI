# User Personas: Hospital Platform

This document defines the primary archetypal personas representing key user segments interacting with the hospital website.

---

### Persona 1: Rajesh Verma - The Emergency Attendant
- **Age:** 38 | **Location:** Sundar Nagar, Raipur | **Device:** Smartphone (Android on 4G)
- **Context:** Rajesh's elderly father collapsed at home with severe chest pain and breathlessness.
- **Goals:** 
  - Quickly find the hospital emergency telephone number to call for advice/ambulance.
  - Check if the ICU and cardiac life support facilities are active 24×7.
  - Get GPS directions to the hospital entrance.
- **Pain Points:** High emotional panic; slow-loading websites with complex menus are unacceptable.
- **Key Features Used:** Topbar Emergency button, Mobile Dock `Emergency` action, Google Maps link.

---

### Persona 2: Sunita Dewangan - The Chronic Kidney Patient's Daughter
- **Age:** 29 | **Location:** Bhilai, Chhattisgarh | **Device:** Laptop & Mobile
- **Context:** Sunita's mother has stage-4 chronic kidney disease and needs ongoing haemodialysis.
- **Goals:**
  - Verify the credentials of the chief nephrologist (Dr. Varun Agrawal).
  - Understand the Apollo Dialysis Clinic setup, hygiene standards, and dialysis slots.
  - Check if Ayushman Bharat covers dialysis sessions.
  - Book an OPD consultation slot for this week.
- **Pain Points:** Unclear doctor availability leading to wasted 40km trips; uncertainty over government scheme coverage.
- **Key Features Used:** Today's OPD Status board, Doctor profile card, Insurance empanelment guide, Appointment booking form.

---

### Persona 3: Manoj Sahu - The Working Professional Getting Routine Lab Reports
- **Age:** 34 | **Location:** Daganiya, Raipur | **Device:** Smartphone
- **Context:** Manoj had routine fever and CBC blood tests conducted at the hospital pathology laboratory this morning.
- **Goals:**
  - Check whether his blood report is ready.
  - Download the PDF report directly to his phone to forward to his physician.
- **Pain Points:** Taking time off work to stand in line at the hospital billing counter.
- **Key Features Used:** Patient Portal (`#patients`), Mobile + PIN report lookup form.

---

### Persona 4: Maya Patel - The Prospective Maternity Patient
- **Age:** 27 | **Location:** Rohinipuram, Raipur | **Device:** iPhone
- **Context:** Maya is pregnant and looking for a reliable, safe hospital with dedicated gynaecologists and a clean labour room.
- **Goals:**
  - Review the qualifications of Dr. Sushma Agrawal (Obstetrician & Gynaecologist).
  - Check hospital infrastructure (dedicated labour room, neonatal care, visiting hours for attendants).
  - Inquire about antenatal packages and cashless insurance options.
- **Pain Points:** Fear of unhygienic environments or rude staff; needs reassurance of NABH accreditation.
- **Key Features Used:** Obstetrics & Gynaecology department details, Facilities page, Visiting hours timetable, Chatbot helpdesk.
