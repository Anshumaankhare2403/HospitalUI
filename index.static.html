Centres of Clinical Excellence

Advanced tertiary clinical programmes combining world-class specialist training with modular surgical and diagnostic infrastructure.

Nephrology & Dialysis

Cashless Insurance & Government Schemes

Please carry these documents to the 24×7 TPA Desk in the main reception lobby:

✓ Original Health Insurance Card or Policy Document

✓ Treating Doctor's Initial Prescription & Referral
